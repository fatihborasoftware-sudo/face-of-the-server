Khoa — the face of the server — on your WordPress site.

== Description ==
Adds two Elementor widgets (category "FB Khoa") and two shortcodes:

* Khoa Hero / [khoa_hero] — full-screen live 3D figure with your title, text and a "Meet Khoa" button that starts his spoken intro.
* Khoa Stage / [khoa_stage] — nine server situations (backup, intrusion, heat, memory, disk, load, update, alarm, normal) driving the full dashboard.

English and Turkish: the language follows Polylang (or the site language). Voice lines are recorded (Piper: en_GB "alan", tr_TR "dfki", shaped as the "voice of the server") and play from the plugin itself; three.js is bundled. All numbers are demo data — nothing connects to a real server. On phones Khoa runs in a lighter mode automatically, and he pauses whenever he is scrolled out of view.

Credits: body mesh "Male Full Body Ecorche" by Diego Luján García (Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0) — keep the credit visible. three.js r128 (MIT). Source: https://github.com/fatihborasoftware-sudo/face-of-the-server
